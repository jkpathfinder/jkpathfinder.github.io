👋 Hi there! I'm a second-year Ph.D. student in Industrial Engineering at the [UbiHealth Lab](https://ubihealth.ise.illinois.edu/), [The Grainger College of Engineering](https://grainger.illinois.edu/), [University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign](https://illinois.edu/), where I am very fortunate to be advised by [Prof. Yiwen Dong](https://yiwen-dong.github.io/). My research is highly interdisciplinary, spanning AI for healthcare, AI-driven clinical decision support systems, human motion analysis, foundation model algorithms, and multimodal sensor data analysis. You can find my CV here: [Mingrui Ma's Curriculum Vitae](/assets/CV__Ma_Mingrui___Looking_For_Internships_.pdf). I hope to exchange ideas with and learn from excellent AI scientists worldwide, and together shape what health intelligence can become.
